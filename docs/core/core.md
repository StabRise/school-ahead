school-ahead is a modern, gamified learning management platform designed specifically for homeschooling and alternative education. It streamlines the entire educational process by bridging the gap between students, tutors, and parents, turning independent learning into a seamless, engaging experience.

## Target Audience
Students: Independent learners who want the freedom to work ahead of schedule, track their personal progress, earn diamond rewards for early completions, and manage their daily tasks without the constraints of rigid, traditional school routines.

Tutors / Mentors: Educators responsible for specific subjects across different classes who need a streamlined dashboard to review student assignments, provide feedback, and handle support requests in real-time.

Parents: Families practicing homeschooling who need reliable oversight of their children's progress, debt tracking, and automated reporting to ensure smooth academic certification.

## Key Value Proposition
school-ahead empowers students to take control of their learning tempo while giving tutors and parents the tools they need to monitor progress, catch up on academic "tails," and celebrate milestones—all wrapped in a clean, motivating environment.
