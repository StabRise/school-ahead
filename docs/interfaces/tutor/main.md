# Tutor Main Screen ("Rapid Response Dashboard")

The primary purpose of this screen is to provide tutors with the ability to instantly identify which students need assistance or whose submissions are awaiting review within seconds, eliminating the need to navigate into each class individually.

## 1. "Need Help" Column / Block
* **Functionality:** Collects and displays real-time notifications whenever a student sets a specific lesson's status to *Need Help* in their schedule.
* **Visible Details:** The tutor immediately sees **who** (student's full name), from **which class**, for **which subject**, and **which exact lesson** caused difficulties, allowing them to quickly provide feedback or reach out.

## 2. "Pending Review" Column / Block
* **Functionality:** Displays a queue of practical assignments and submissions uploaded by students that require grading and evaluation.
* **Tutor Actions:** The tutor can filter submissions by their assigned subjects and classes, open the submission details, inspect uploaded files or answers, assign a final grade (1–12 scale or Pass/Fail), and leave direct feedback or comments.
