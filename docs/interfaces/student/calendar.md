# Weekly Calendar (Student Main Screen)

## 1. Weekly Grid
* **Functionality:** Displays scheduled lessons organized in columns for each day of the week (Monday through Sunday), featuring intuitive navigation to easily switch between past, current, and future weeks.

## 2. Backlog Block ("Tails" Prevention)
* **Functionality:** A unique mechanism designed to prevent the accumulation of academic backlogs. If a lesson is not completed on its scheduled day, it does not disappear or get lost.
* **Automatic Transfer:** Unfinished lessons automatically migrate into a dedicated **Backlog** block on the current week's interface (labeled with their original date, e.g., *Mon #4, Tue #2*), allowing the student to catch up and clear their debts flexibly at a later time.
